Wind Turbine

Study of wind-turbine systems with a focus on the mechanical powertrain, rotating components, bearings, and structural behaviour.

System Overview

Basic power transmission path:

Wind → Rotor → Main Shaft → Bearings → Gearbox → Generator

The goal is to understand how loads and motion travel through the complete mechanical system.
