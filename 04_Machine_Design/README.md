# Machine Design

Design and analysis of mechanical components and systems under real engineering loads.

## Topics

* Mechanical Design Fundamentals
* Design for Strength and Stiffness
* Shafts
* Bearings
* Gears
* Fasteners and Bolted Joints
* Couplings
* Springs
* Fatigue Design
* Failure Theories
* Preload and Contact
* Tolerances and Fits

## Engineering Applications

Focus on applications involving:

* Bearing and shaft systems
* Bearing housings
* Gearboxes
* Wind-turbine powertrains
* Rotating machinery
* Bolted and preloaded joints
* FEA-based design validation
